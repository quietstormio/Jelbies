# Jelmiko Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-09. Jelmiko is made by MGS LLC.

In short: Jelmiko works offline and never sends anything anywhere.

## No data collected
The game never connects to our servers or anyone else's. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We do not collect any data about you or your child.

## Microphone and voice
The microphone is only used while you hold the mic button, or while the optional always-on Ears setting is on. What you say is turned into words on the phone by Apple's on-device speech recognition. It is never recorded to a file or sent anywhere. Apple may download its speech model once over the internet. That download is done by iOS, not by Jelmiko, and no game data goes with it.

## Photos
Photos taken in Photo mode stay inside the app. They only leave it if you choose to save them to your Photos or share them.

## Grown-up check
Anything that leaves the game is behind a grown-up hold button: sharing photos, opening the phone's Settings or Shortcuts, and emailing Support.

## Names and saves
The names you type and everything your Jelmiko have earned stay on this phone.

## Widgets and Live Activities
Widgets and Live Activities show your Jelmiko's names, moods and pictures on your own Home Screen and Lock Screen. That information stays on your phone.

## Park visitors
On phones that support Apple Intelligence, the text for Park visitors (their names and what they say) may be written on the phone by Apple Intelligence. It is asked to keep everything gentle and kid-friendly, and the result is checked and filtered before it is shown. Phones without it use words written in advance.

## Delete everything
Open Settings and tap Reset save to erase your Jelmiko, JellyCoins, items and photos from the app. Or delete the app from your phone. Photos you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them.
