# Jelmiko Help & Support

## How to play
- **Feed:** tap the heart, then Feed, and pick a snack.
- **Drink:** tap the heart, then Drink.
- **Power-ups:** some foods give a boost for a while. The Shop shows which.
- **Favourite food:** every Jelmiko loves one food. Its power-up lasts twice as long.
- **Clean:** tap your Jelmiko, then Clean, to scrub, rinse and shine.
- **Play:** tap Play and pick a toy.
- **Call by name:** hold the mic button and say your Jelmiko's name. With Tap to talk on, tap the mic instead.
- **Jelly Race, Jelly Show and Jelly Volleyball:** go out to compete and earn JellyCoins.

## The mic is not working
A grown-up can turn the microphone on in your phone's Settings, under Jelmiko. Turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.

The first time you use your voice, the phone needs to download a small speech pack. Stay online once, then it works offline.

## How do I start over?
Open Settings in the game and tap Reset save, then hold the red button. This erases your Jelmiko, JellyCoins and items for good.
