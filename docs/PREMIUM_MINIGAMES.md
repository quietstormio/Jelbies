# Premium mini games (planning)

Status: documented only, not built.

## Idea

More mini games will be buyable as a premium feature. The premium unlock is a real-money purchase (StoreKit, one-time).
The rest of the game stays offline-only, and the in-game currency (JellyCoins / Drops) stays earn-only: it can never be bought.
It is a kids game, so there is no pressure buying, no ads, no timers, and a parental gate on the purchase sheet.

These stay free. Prizes still pay earn-only coins.

## Unlock model

- Per game: each premium game is its own purchase and shows a preview card in the game picker.
- Optional bundle: all premium games at a lower price.
- A premium game, once bought, earns and levels like the free ones (coins by result, no pay-to-win items).
- Entitlements are checked locally from the App Store receipt, so the game still works offline.

## Candidate premium games

Every one is body-only: tiny jellies with stubby arms and bean feet. They roll, bounce, shove, hop and lean. They never reach or carry with their hands.

1. Jelly Sumo: belly-bump two jellies out of a ring (shove with the body, no grabbing).
2. Bounce Hill: hop up wobbly platforms, the squash and stretch sets the jump height.
3. Bumper Pond: float on a pond and nudge a big ball into the goal with bellies and heads.
4. Pin Roll: bowl a round jelly friend down a lane at pins, tilt-steer.
5. Cloud Hop: tap-timed hops over clouds to collect stars (a gentle runner).
6. Tug Wobble: lean-and-dig tug of war, pulling with a belly harness (no hands).
