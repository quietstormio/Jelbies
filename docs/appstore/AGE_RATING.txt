----------------------------------------------------------------------
IN-APP CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Parental Controls            -> No.   Reason: the grown-up hold button (UI/ParentGate.swift) only blocks a child from leaving the app (share sheet, system Settings, Shortcuts, the support email button). It does not limit or filter anything for a child. If the form's wording makes you want to say Yes, that does not change the rating.
Age Assurance                -> No.   Reason: no age checks, no accounts, no Declared Age Range use anywhere in the source.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CAPABILITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Unrestricted Web Access      -> No.   Reason: no web view and no browser anywhere in the code (checked: no WKWebView, no SFSafariViewController). The only things the app opens outside itself are the system Settings, the Shortcuts app and a mail link, all behind the hold button.
User-Generated Content       -> No.   Reason: the player types names for their own Jelmiko; they stay on the device and are never shown to anyone else through the app.
Messaging and Chat           -> No.   Reason: no player-to-player communication of any kind. Park friends are computer-controlled visitors.
Social Media / sharing feed  -> No.   Reason: none. A photo can leave only through the system share sheet, behind the hold button.
Advertising                  -> No.   Reason: no ad code; the app never connects to a server.

Alcohol, Tobacco or Drug Use or References       -> None.   Reason: the Cafe sells pretend treats (a Jelly puff, a Baby latte) for the Jelmiko. Nothing refers to people drinking alcohol or using tobacco or drugs.

MADE FOR KIDS / KIDS CATEGORY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Answer: No.  The owner decided on 2026-11-08 that the app is NOT in the Kids Category (it would limit what the app may do and the hold gate was added instead).
What choosing No means here (verify in App Store Connect and App Review Guidelines):
- You are not subject to the extra Kids Category rules (no third-party analytics or ads, a parental gate before links or purchases, etc.). This app already has no analytics, ads, links or purchases, and has the hold gate in front of everything that leaves the app.
- The app is not listed in the Kids section of the store, and the store's "made for kids" badge is not shown.
- The listing must not say the app is in the Kids Category or "made for kids" (the text in LISTING.txt does not).
- A children's app that is not in the Kids Category still has to follow Apple's rules on data from children (Guideline 5.1.4) and the law (for example COPPA, GDPR-K). The app collects no data, so there is nothing to put at risk; this is also why privacy.html says in plain words that nothing is collected.
- The rating (4+) is separate from the category. You can have a 4+ app that is not in the Kids Category.

- "Does your app have an Age Rating override or a higher minimum age?" Leave at the calculated rating.
- Declared Age Range (Apple API): not used by the app; no answer needed.
