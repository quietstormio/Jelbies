Jelmiko Help and Support

Answers to the questions we hear most. The same help is in the game under Settings, Help & Support. Last updated 15 November 2026.

How do I play?

  • Feed: tap the food button and pick a snack.
  • Drink: tap the drink button to fill a thirsty Jelmiko.
  • Clean: tap Clean to scrub, rinse and make them sparkle.
  • Play: tap Play and pick a toy.
  • Call by name: hold the mic button and say your Jelmiko's name.
  • Jelly Race, Jelly Show and Jelly Volleyball: go out to town to compete and earn JellyCoins.

You start with one Jelmiko, the Jelly Spring's welcome gift. You can bring home up to three. More are adopted at the Jelly Spring in town, using JellyCoins you have earned by playing. JellyCoins cannot be bought with real money.

I can't hear anything

  • Check the silent switch or Silent Mode on your device. Jelmiko follows it, so the game is quiet when your device is on silent. Turn silent off, or use the volume buttons.
  • In the game, open Settings (the gear) and check that Music and Sound effects are switched on and not turned down. The Jelmiko voices follow the Sound effects volume.
  • Check that your device's volume is up and that headphones or a Bluetooth speaker are not taking the sound.
  • If the sound still stays away, close the game completely and open it again.

The microphone is not working

Voice is optional, and the rest of the game works without it. If you would like it on, a grown-up can open your device's Settings app, find Jelmiko in the list of apps, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. The game can also take you there from its voice message, after a grown-up check (press and hold the button for two seconds).

The first time you use your voice, the device may need to download a small Apple speech pack. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data once, tap the mic again, and after that voice works without the internet.

Voice turns what you say into words on your device. It is not recorded and not sent anywhere.

How do I start a new game?

Open Settings (the gear) in the game, tap Reset save, then press and hold the red button. The game then plays its opening and lets you pick your first Jelmiko again.

Your old save is replaced only once you have chosen your new first Jelmiko. If you back out before that, the old game is still there. Once replaced, your Jelmiko, JellyCoins, items and photos in the game are gone for good, so please be sure.

How does saving work?

The game saves by itself as you play and when you leave it. There is nothing to press. Your save lives on your device only: there is no account and no cloud. If you move to a new phone or iPad, use your device's normal backup and restore (iCloud or a computer) to bring the game and its save with you. If you delete the app without a backup, the save is deleted with it.

While the game is closed, time still passes gently, so your Jelmiko may be a little hungry or sleepy when you come back. Your Jelmiko are never taken away.

My photos

Photo mode keeps your pictures inside the game, in the album on the main menu. To keep one in your own Photos library, open it and tap Save to Photos; the device will ask once for permission to add pictures. Sharing a photo asks a grown-up first.

Does Jelmiko need the internet?

No. There are no accounts and no ads, and the game does not connect to anything. The only time the internet may be used is the one-time Apple speech pack download described above, and your device does that.
