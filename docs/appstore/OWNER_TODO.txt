Prepared 2026-11-15. Written for someone who has not shipped an app before. Button names are from my memory of Apple's websites and Xcode: they move around, so if a label differs, look for the closest one (verify).
Tags:  [YOU ONLY] = needs your Apple ID, payment, legal choice or a decision.   [HELPER OK] = someone with access to your Mac (a developer, or Claude Code on your Mac) can do it for you.
Files for you to paste from are in this folder: LISTING.txt, AGE_RATING.txt, APP_PRIVACY.txt, REVIEW_NOTES.txt, privacy.html, support.html, SCREENSHOT_PLAN.txt.
Identifiers to type exactly (they look like old names; that is correct, they are internal): App bundle ID  io.mgsllc.BobV1   Widget bundle ID  io.mgsllc.BobV1.JelbiesWidget   App group  group.io.mgsllc.BobV1   Team ID  5VVYA96U3R   The player-facing name is Jelmiko.

BEFORE ANY OF THIS: two things in the code are not yours to do but must be done before the build goes up (from the 11-15 audit, docs and notes: project-release-audit-2026-11-15):
 B1  The approved jelly look must be switched on properly (JellyLook.swift, a code fix). [HELPER OK]

Do not upload until B1 and B2 are done.

STEPS
 1. Apple Developer Program membership active. [YOU ONLY] Go to developer.apple.com > Account, sign in with your Apple ID; it must say "Apple Developer Program" with a future renewal date and show Team ID 5VVYA96U3R. Membership is a yearly fee paid to Apple. Because the seller is MGS LLC (an organisation), the account must be an Organization account (it needs a D-U-N-S number); if you joined as an individual, the seller name shown in the store will be your own name (verify with Apple if unsure).
 2. Accept agreements. [YOU ONLY] appstoreconnect.apple.com > Business (or Agreements, Tax, and Banking) > accept the "Paid Apps" agreement if you will charge a price; a free app needs only the free-apps agreement. Fill bank and tax details only if the app is paid.
 3. Signing: the simpler way is Xcode's automatic signing. [HELPER OK, but your Apple ID must be signed in to Xcode: Xcode > Settings > Accounts > + > Apple ID, once, YOU ONLY] In Xcode, click the project BobV1 > each target (BobV1 and JelbiesWidget) > Signing & Capabilities > tick "Automatically manage signing" and choose team 5VVYA96U3R. When you archive and export for App Store, Xcode creates the Apple Distribution certificate and the App Store provisioning profile for you. Making the certificate by hand on developer.apple.com is only needed if automatic signing fails. Note from the audit: your Mac's keychain currently has only an "Apple Development" certificate, so the first export will have to create the Distribution one; the account needs the Admin or App Manager role to allow that.
 4. Register the identifiers and the app group. Automatic signing creates the two App IDs (io.mgsllc.BobV1 and io.mgsllc.BobV1.JelbiesWidget) and the app group group.io.mgsllc.BobV1 for you when both targets have the App Groups capability. If you prefer to check by hand: developer.apple.com > Certificates, IDs & Profiles > Identifiers: both App IDs must list "App Groups" with group.io.mgsllc.BobV1 ticked. [HELPER OK for Xcode; YOU ONLY if it must be done on the website]
 5. Create the app record. [YOU ONLY] appstoreconnect.apple.com > Apps > the blue "+" > New App. Platforms: iOS (iPad is included automatically). Name: Jelmiko. Primary Language: English (U.S.). Bundle ID: choose io.mgsllc.BobV1 from the list (it appears after step 4 or after the first upload; if it is not listed, register it first). SKU: jelmiko-001 (any private text). User Access: Full Access. Click Create.
 6. Host the two pages. [YOU ONLY, or HELPER OK if they have access to your web host] Upload privacy.html and support.html (in this folder) to any web host as they are: for example your MGS LLC website, GitHub Pages or any static host. They have no scripts and no other files. Keep both in the same folder so the link on the support page to the privacy page works. Open each address in a phone's browser to confirm it displays. Note the two addresses.
 7. Paste the two addresses. [YOU ONLY] App Store Connect > your app > App Information (left menu, under General): "Privacy Policy URL" = the privacy.html address. Then on the version page (step 9) "Support URL" = the support.html address. Save.
 8. App Privacy. [YOU ONLY] App Store Connect > your app > App Privacy > Get Started > answer "No, we do not collect data from this app" > Save > Publish. Details: APP_PRIVACY.txt.
 9. Age rating. [YOU ONLY] App Information > Age Rating > Edit (or Set Up Age Ratings) > answer each question from AGE_RATING.txt (all None / No) > Save. Expect 4+. Do NOT tick Made for Kids / the Kids Category.
10. Category. [YOU ONLY] App Information > Category: Primary = Games, Secondary = Entertainment; game subgenres if asked: Simulation, Casual (verify). See LISTING.txt section 8.
11. Price: it is not decided in the project notes (docs/RELEASE-1.0.md says "pricing (TBD by the user)"; an older 10-19 note mentioned a pay-once idea, but nothing was ever set, and the app has no in-app purchases). [YOU ONLY] App Store Connect > your app > Pricing and Availability > Price Schedule > choose "Free" or a price tier. Please decide: Free, or one fixed price. Do not guess from this file.
12. Availability. [YOU ONLY] Pricing and Availability > App Availability > choose the countries (all is the default). Remember the age rating is calculated per region. Also choose how it is released after approval on the version page: manually, automatically, or phased.
13. Paste the listing texts. [YOU ONLY] App Store > the version "1.0 Prepare for Submission": Promotional Text, Description, Keywords, Support URL, Copyright, What's New from LISTING.txt; the Subtitle and the Name go in App Information > Localizable Information. Count characters are given next to each field.
14. Screenshots. [HELPER OK to capture; YOU ONLY to approve and upload] Follow SCREENSHOT_PLAN.txt. Upload to the version page > Screenshots: iPhone 6.9-inch set and iPad 13-inch set. Drag in the files in order.
15. App icon. The icon (AppIcon) is part of the build: it is in the app's Assets. The 1024 x 1024 marketing icon is taken from the build, you do not upload it separately. Check Xcode > the app target > the AppIcon set looks right. [HELPER OK]
16. App Review Information. [YOU ONLY] On the version page scroll to App Review Information: Sign-in required = off; your contact details; paste the Notes from REVIEW_NOTES.txt. Export compliance: see REVIEW_NOTES.txt section B.
17. Raise the build number. [HELPER OK] Every upload needs a build number App Store Connect has not seen for version 1.0. The project has CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION = 1 in every target: for the first upload that is fine; for every later upload raise it in every target at once, for example the command  agvtool new-version -all 2  in the project folder (or edit project.pbxproj).
18. Build and upload. [HELPER OK; needs your Apple ID in Xcode] In Xcode (released, not beta): choose "Any iOS Device (arm64)" as the destination > Product > Archive > in the Organizer click "Validate App", then "Generate Privacy Report" (check both manifests appear), then "Distribute App" > App Store Connect > Upload. Or with the command line: xcodebuild archive then xcodebuild -exportArchive with an export options plist (method app-store-connect, team 5VVYA96U3R, automatic signing). Make sure the dev arguments are off (B2) and the launch log never prints "DEV ARGS ENABLED".
19. Wait for processing. Roughly 10 to 60 minutes after upload the build appears in App Store Connect > your app > TestFlight (you get an email). Fix anything the email says.
20. Test it. [YOU ONLY] Install the build from TestFlight on a real phone (or the second device / iPad, never by deleting the app on your own phone without backing up the save): new game shows 40 JellyCoins and the first-day tour; no "(test)" pickers in Settings; permission prompts say Jelmiko; the look matches what you approved. The audit lists more checks (project-release-audit-2026-11-15, "On-device checklist before upload").
21. Select the build. [YOU ONLY] Version page > Build > "+" > pick the processed build.
22. Check everything is green. [YOU ONLY] Fix any red warnings (missing screenshots, missing URLs, privacy not published).
23. Submit. [YOU ONLY] Click "Add for Review" then "Submit to App Review". Review usually takes about one to three days (verify). If they reply with a question, answer in the same thread (App Store Connect > App Review > the message) using REVIEW_NOTES.txt.
24. Before you click submit, read OPEN_QUESTIONS.txt: the sound licence and the name search are not settled and can only be settled by you.
