4. DESCRIPTION  (limit 4000)
======================================================================
----- start of text (copy everything between the lines) -----
Meet your Jelmiko: baby jellies from Wobble Isle who need someone to look after them.

You have just moved to Wobble Isle, a little floating town in the sky. In the middle of town is the Jelly Spring, where a few dewdrops wobble awake as Jelmiko every morning. The Spring has a welcome gift for its newest neighbour: your first Jelmiko, free. Choose Bob (blue), Pip (yellow, with a pointed tail and orange spikes), Moss (green, with two wobbly antennas) or Lavender (purple, with little wings). Later, with JellyCoins you have earned, you can adopt more friends from the Spring, including Teddy, Pearl and Panda. Up to three Jelmiko can live at home with you.

LOOK AFTER THEM
Feed them snacks, fill their drinks, give them a bubbly clean until they sparkle, and enjoy a gentle squish and a cuddle. Jelmiko yawn, stretch, wander, nap, make friends and notice each other, so it is lovely just to sit and watch them. Each one has its own personality and its own little voice.

PLAY AND DRESS UP
Pick a toy and play together. Dress your Jelmiko in hats, glasses, shoes and more, and take pictures in Photo mode. Choose a theme colour for the game, with a matching app icon if you like.

GO OUT IN TOWN
Visit the Shop and the Café, relax at the Jelly Spa, and make friends in the Park. Invite a friend over for a play date or a sleepover.

COMPETE AND EARN JELLYCOINS
Jelly Race: run curved courses with two foot buttons and jump the hurdles.
Jelly Show: show off your Jelmiko's look, dance and tricks.
Jelly Volleyball: a 2 v 2 game on the beach where Jelmiko bump, dig and spike with their bodies.
Train up, win ribbons and trophies for your shelf, and spend your JellyCoins in town. JellyCoins are earned by playing. They cannot be bought.

TALK TO THEM
Hold the mic button and say your Jelmiko's name or a trick word. Your voice is turned into words on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. The mic is optional: everything else in the game works without it.

CALM AND PRIVATE
- Works offline once installed. No account or sign-in.
- No ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
- Anything that leaves the game (sharing a photo, opening the system Settings, or emailing support) is behind a grown-up hold button.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
A widget shows how your Jelmiko are doing, and Live Activities can follow a race or bedtime.

Questions? dione@mgsllc.io
----- end of text -----

- Wear slots (hats, faces, shoes, necks, hands, bodies): StoreCatalog.swift WearSlot. Photo mode: Settings > Game > Photo mode.
- Theme colour + 'Match app icon to theme': Settings > Appearance (SettingsScreen.swift).
- JellyCoins earn-only, no real-money path: StoreCatalog.swift header comment; no StoreKit anywhere in the source.
- Voice on device: Voice/SpeechInput.swift (DictationTranscriber + SpeechAnalyzer); mic permission text in project.pbxproj.

Siri phrases, ratings or ages, awards.
'Works offline once installed' is used instead of 'never online' because iOS may download Apple's speech pack once when the mic is first used (done by the system, not by the game).
The description names no price. Price is undecided (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.txt).
