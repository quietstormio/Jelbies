Jelmiko Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026. Jelmiko is made by MGS LLC.

In short

Jelmiko works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it does not collect any information about you or your child. Everything the game remembers stays on your own phone or iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. The game never sends anything to us or to anyone else: it contains no code to connect to a server, and it has no third-party software inside it that could collect data. We cannot see your name, your child's name, your Jelmiko, your photos or anything else you do in the game.

What stays on your device

Your save (your Jelmiko, the names you give them, the JellyCoins they have earned, items, trophies and settings) is stored on your device only. If you use device backups (iCloud or a computer), your device may include the game's files in its backup. That is a feature of the phone or iPad, not something we receive.

Microphone and voice

You can talk to your Jelmiko by pressing and holding the microphone button and saying a name or a trick word. There is also an optional listening setting called Ears, which is off until you turn it on. The microphone is used only for these two things.

What you say is turned into words on the device by Apple's built-in speech recognition. The sound is not recorded to a file and is not sent anywhere. The game keeps only a small note for each Jelmiko of which word you use for its name or a trick and how well it has learned it. That note stays on the device.

The first time you use voice, iOS may download Apple's speech language pack over the internet. Your phone or iPad does this, not the game, and no information from the game goes with it. After that, voice works without the internet.

Microphone and speech recognition are optional. If you say no, or switch them off later in the device's Settings, the rest of the game works as before.

Photos

Photos taken in Photo mode are kept inside the game. They leave the game only if you choose to save one to your Photos library or share it. The game asks permission only to add pictures to your library; it cannot see the pictures already there. The game does not use the camera.

Grown-up check

Sharing a photo, opening the device's Settings or Shortcuts app from the game, and the "Write to us" email button are all behind a grown-up check: a button that must be pressed and held for two seconds.

Widgets and Live Activities

The widget and Live Activities show your Jelmiko's names, moods and pictures on your own Home Screen and Lock Screen. This information is shared between the game and its widget on your device only.

Park visitors

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the names and short lines of the visitors you meet in the Park may be written on the device by Apple's on-device model, and the result is filtered before it is shown. Other devices use words written in advance. Nothing is sent off the device.

Children

Jelmiko is a gentle game that children can enjoy. Because it collects no information at all, there is no child's data for us to hold, use or share. There are no accounts, no chat, no ads and no real-money purchases in the game.

Deleting everything

In the game, open Settings and tap Reset save, then hold the red button, to erase your Jelmiko, JellyCoins, items and photos from the game. You can also delete the app from your device. Pictures you saved to your own Photos library stay there until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
