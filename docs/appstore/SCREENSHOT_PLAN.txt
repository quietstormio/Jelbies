JELMIKO 1.0 - SCREENSHOT PLAN (nothing has been captured; this is a plan)
Prepared 2026-11-15. Sizes are from my memory of Apple's specification page: verify in App Store Connect (the "Screenshot specifications" help page) before exporting.

======================================================================
1. SIZES (verify)
======================================================================
- 1 to 10 screenshots per device size; .jpeg, .jpg or .png; NO alpha channel / transparency; RGB.
- iPhone: required group is the largest iPhone size you provide. Use the 6.9-inch set: 1320 x 2868 px portrait (landscape 2868 x 1320). Also accepted: 1290 x 2796, 1260 x 2736. App Store Connect scales the smaller iPhone sizes down from the 6.9-inch set when the layout is the same. If it will not accept it for the required slot, add the 6.3-inch size: 1206 x 2622 (landscape 2622 x 1206).
- iPad (required, because the app runs on iPad): 13-inch set: 2064 x 2752 px portrait (landscape 2752 x 2064), or 2048 x 2732 (landscape 2732 x 2048). The 11-inch iPad sizes are optional and scaled from the 13-inch set.
- Check each file for alpha: for example, in Terminal, "sips -g hasAlpha file.png" must say "no". To flatten: open in Preview > File > Export, untick Alpha.
- Optional: an app preview video (up to 3 per size). Not planned: the intro video is a logo sting and is not gameplay.

======================================================================
2. HOW TO CAPTURE (for whoever does it; do not use a build with dev arguments)
======================================================================
- Use a normal build from phone-jelly-final with the owner's approved jelly look, in the Simulator at the sizes above (iPhone 17 Pro Max class, iPad Pro 13-inch class) or on a real device and crop.
- Save: three Jelmiko at home (for example Bob blue, Pip yellow, Lavender purple), some trophies and a comfortable JellyCoins balance; daytime in the room.
- Use portrait for all iPhone shots. For iPad use portrait for 2 shots (1 and 8) and landscape for the rest, or all portrait; keep one orientation per set so the gallery looks tidy.
- Older candidates exist in scratchpad/appstore/{ipad13,iphone69,iphone61} from 2026-11-09. They PREDATE the rename to Jelmiko and the jelly look: do not use them; they show the old name and old body look.
- Shots must show real game screens only. Captions (optional) are added in a design tool afterwards, 4 words or fewer.
- Keep the bottom buttons clear: in portrait a front Jelmiko can sit under the bottom buttons (a known item in RELEASE-1.0.md). Re-pose and re-take any shot where a Jelmiko is covered.

======================================================================
3. SHOT LIST (8 shots, same story for iPhone and 13-inch iPad, in this order)
======================================================================
1. Screen: the home room, three Jelmiko doing something lively (one waving, one walking, one sitting). Creatures: Bob, Pip, Lavender. Moment: daytime, no menu open. Caption: "Meet your Jelmiko" (3 words).
2. Screen: feeding. Creatures: one Jelmiko with a snack tossed toward its mouth. Moment: the snack mid-air or just caught. Caption: "Snack time together" (3).
3. Screen: the bubbly clean. Creatures: Moss (green, antennas) covered in soap bubbles with sparkles. Moment: bubbles on, a sparkle burst. Caption: "Bubbles and sparkles" (3).
4. Screen: dress up (a hat and glasses on a Jelmiko). Creatures: Lavender with wings visible plus a hat, or Pip with a hat. Moment: the Jelmiko wearing the look, not the shop list. Caption: "Dress them up" (3).
5. Screen: the town map "Where to?" with the places. Moment: the map as it first opens. Caption: "Explore Wobble Isle" (3).
6. Screen: Jelly Race in progress: a curved course with the player's Jelmiko and the other racers mid-run. Moment: a hurdle jump if possible. Caption: "Race for JellyCoins" (3).
7. Screen: Jelly Volleyball on the beach. Creatures: two on each side, ball in the air. Moment: a belly-flop spike or a head bonk. Caption: "Bump, dig, spike" (3).
8. Screen: the Jelly Spring (adoption) with the choice of friends on the stage. Creatures: the species shown on the stage. Moment: one Jelmiko hopped forward. Caption: "Adopt new friends" (3).
Spare (use instead of 2 or 3 if a shot comes out poorly): a sleepy night room with a Jelmiko napping ("Cosy bedtime"); Photo mode viewfinder ("Snap a photo"); the widget on a Home Screen ("Jelmiko on your Home Screen").

AVOID in screenshots (being changed now, or not yet checked):
- The Jelly Show screens and its contestant line-up (the contestants are being changed).
- The Park: a toy (seesaw) is being removed and the visitors are being changed. A plain room, town, race, beach and Spring are safer.
- The Shop screens (the decor / furniture and toy tabs are being changed; the Shop has no furniture section and no RC car, scooter, frisbee or bubble machine to show).
- Any Settings, Privacy or debug screen; any "(test)" picker; any screen that shows 10,000 coins (a store build starts at 40; use a believable balance).
- Landscape of the menu, adoption and photo screens until they have been checked on the iPad landscape simulator (RELEASE-1.0.md lists them as unchecked).
