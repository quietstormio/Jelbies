# Jelmiko

Jelmiko is a calm virtual pet game for iPhone and iPad. You look after up to three soft, wobbly jelly creatures (each one is a Jelmiko): feed them, bathe them, cuddle them, teach them tricks, dress them up, and take them out to the park, the café and three kinds of competition. Much of the game is simply watching them: they wander the room, nap, play with toys and with each other, and make friends.

The game is fully offline. There are no accounts, no ads, no purchases with real money and no tracking.

- **Platform:** iPhone and iPad, iOS / iPadOS 26.2 or later, portrait and landscape
- **Version:** 1.0, in development
- **Language:** English

> A note on names: the game was first called "Jelbies" and its first creature is called Bob, so the repository, the Xcode project and many identifiers still say `Jelbies`, `BobV1`, `Blob` or `Jelly`. On screen it is always Jelmiko.

## The Jelmiko

- **Up to three at home**, and up to six more resting at **Jelmiko Camp**. You can swap who is home, bring one back, or say goodbye for good.
- **Your first Jelmiko is a gift from the Jelly Spring.** You choose one of the four starters:
  - **Blueberry**: plain and perfectly round
  - **Lemon Drop**: soft spikes and a tail
  - **Taro**: little wings
  - **Matcha**: two wobbly antennas
- **More kinds to adopt later**, 25 in all in this build: bears with round ears (Teddy, Panda), a white-winged Pearl, gradients (Opal, Sunset, Cotton Candy, Ocean, Lagoon, Lemon-Lime), cow prints (Moo, Cocoa, Blush), Marble, Midnight, and metals and jewels (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire, Amethyst).
- **Adoption at the Jelly Spring:** read about a kind and spin its 3D model, then pick one of three, each with its own personality, favourite snack and, for the starters, a slightly different shade. Now and then a starter turns up in a rare jewel colour.
- **Eight personalities:** Bouncy, Cuddly, Curious, Sleepy, Giggly, Brave, Gentle and Cheeky. Personality shapes what a Jelmiko likes to eat and how it behaves.
- **Every Jelmiko shares one soft body.** Kinds differ by colour, markings and one feature (wings, a tail and spikes, antennas, bear ears).

## Looking after them

- **Three needs: Food, Drink and Love**, plus how clean they are. A Jelmiko's Profile describes each in plain words (Full, Hungry, Quenched, Thirsty, A bit grubby ...).
- **Meals and drinks.** A tray appears and everyone eats together. Each Jelmiko has a favourite food.
- **Bath time** in the room: soap, rinse and shine, with a choice of soaps.
- **Jelly Spa** in town for a wash and pamper, in three tiers.
- **Cuddles.** Call one over for a close-up: stroke it, pat its head, boop a cheek, give it a squish, pick it up. It purrs, and hearts float up.
- **Sleep and the day.** The game has its own day and night (or it can follow the real clock). Jelmiko get sleepy, put on sleep masks and go to bed; you can skip to morning.
- **Time away is gentle.** Needs fall slowly while you are gone and never to the bottom. They come to greet you when you return.
- **Twelve power-ups** (Cozy, Sugar rush, Focused, Full belly, Shiny, Tidy, Deep sleep, Bouncy, Social, Fresh, Bubbly, Snuggly) come from foods, drinks, café treats, soaps and the Spa.

## Relationship and the Bond trick

- Each Jelmiko has a **Relationship** with you that only grows: Just met, Getting to know you, Friends, Good friends, Close friends, Best friends. It is shown as five hearts on the Profile.
- It grows through everything you do together: meals (more for a favourite food), drinks, baths, cuddles, play, tricks, and outings and competitions.
- At **Best friends** a Jelmiko learns **Bond**: it walks to the front, opens its arms and gives you a hug, with hearts streaming from the top of its head.

## Life at home

- **They roam.** Jelmiko wander the whole floor, stop to look around, break into a run, nap, and meet up for pair and group moments (hugs, dances, a conga line), with more than a hundred idle and social behaviours between them.
- **Ball play.** Flick a ball into the room (bouncy ball, soccer ball, beach ball or hacky sack) and they head it and kick it back.
- **Bubble wand.** Blow bubbles for them to chase and pop.
- **Toys they use by themselves:** a soccer ball and mini goal (kicks, dribbles, shots and passes between two), blocks, a spinning top, a yarn ball, a drum, a trampoline pad, plush toys, a pinwheel, a mini skateboard and a basketball hoop.
- **Tricks.** Sit, spin, hop and jumping jacks from the start, and fifteen more to learn from the Shop (flip, moonwalk, headstand, belly drum, hula, take a bow ...).
- **Voice.** Hold the mic and say a Jelmiko's name or a trick. They also understand "come here", "goodnight", "wake up", praise and more. You can teach nicknames. Speech is recognised on the device.
- **Music Ears.** An unlock that lets them dance to music playing in the room and react to claps, whistles and laughter.
- **Guests.** Invite a park friend over for a **play date** or a **sleepover**.
- **Weather.** Now and then a thunderstorm rolls past the windows and they huddle to watch it.

## Going out

The Town map leads to ten places.

- **Shop:** food, treats, drinks, soaps, clothes, tricks, toys, extra rooms and picture frames, all for JellyCoins. Items can be sold back from the Bag.
- **Café:** take everyone out and order a treat for each from a menu of eight. Food fills hunger, drinks fill thirst, and every treat gives a power-up. After eating they stay at the table and chat. Other Jelmiko are sometimes at their own table. A stamp card makes every eighth order free.
- **Park:** a playground with a slide and a hopscotch court, where your Jelmiko meet visitors. Each visitor builds a friendship with each of your Jelmiko: Stranger, Pal, Friend, Best friend. Friends sometimes bring a gift.
- **Jelly Spa**, **Jelmiko Camp** and the **Jelly Spring** (see above), and a **Friends** page for inviting guests.
- **Jelly Race, Jelly Show and Jelly Volleyball** (below).

## Competitions

Competitions are how JellyCoins are earned.

- **Jelly Race.** Five courses, from a straight sprint to a course with ten hurdles: Puddle, Ripple, Splash, Wave and Tsunami. Winning one opens the next. Training points raise a Jelmiko's speed and focus.
- **Jelly Show.** A daily theme (Sweet Tea Party, Starlight Gala, Beach Games, Cool Cats, Bunny Picnic) and three rounds: **Look** (the outfit against the theme), **Dance** (tap on the beat) and **Trick** (choose three tricks). Four ranks: Puddle, Pond, Lagoon and Ocean.
- **Jelly Volleyball.** Two against two on the beach, with one of your other Jelmiko or a park friend as your teammate. Four courts: Sandcastle, Shore, Reef and Tidal.
- **Ribbons, cups and trophies** for all three. Volleyball trophies carry both teammates' names. Trophies stand on the **Trophy Shelf** on the room's back wall, each with a plaque, and are listed on the winner's Profile.

## Dress up

- **About seventy things to wear** across six slots: hats, face, neck, hands, body and shoes.
- **Dress up for the Jelly Show** on its own screen, with your Jelmiko shown whole from hat to shoes. What it wears for the show is a show outfit: afterwards it goes back to what it wore before.
- Each piece has a style (cute, sweet, sparkly, cool, sporty) that the judges match against the day's theme.

## The home

- **Three rooms:** the Living room, the Sunny Loft and the Cosy Cabin.
- **Arrange the room:** drag toys and furniture into place.
- **Wall picture:** hang one of your own photos in a frame.
- **Photo mode:** move the camera, choose a pose, a filter and a frame, and keep photos in an in-game album.

## Screens

- **Profile** for each Jelmiko: its picture, kind and personality, how it is right now in plain words, its Relationship hearts, favourite food, best friend at the Park, power-ups, and trophies. Notes about its housemates appear as the game gets to know them.
- **Settings:** eight theme colours with matching app icons, music and sound, haptics, a full accessibility page, an option to follow the real clock, and more.
- **Tips and a first-day tour** introduce each part of the game once.

## Outside the app

- **Widgets** for the Home Screen and Lock Screen show how your Jelmiko are doing; one has a button to give them a drink.
- **Live Activities** follow a race or a nap.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** check on the Jelmiko, ask whether they are asleep or hungry, feed them, give them water, say goodnight, or start a race.

## Private by design

- **Nothing leaves the device.** The game makes no network requests. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.
- **JellyCoins are earned by playing** and cannot be bought.
- **Voice is recognised on the device** and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
- **On-device intelligence, where the device supports it,** helps understand loosely phrased voice requests and can write names and greetings for park visitors, checked and filtered before anything is shown. An optional setting lets it write the short notes about housemates on the Profile; that setting is off by default, and pre-written notes are used otherwise.

See [docs/PRIVACY.md](docs/PRIVACY.md).

## Accessibility

VoiceOver labels and actions, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, sound captions and longer hints; easier race controls (forgiving, one button or auto run), help over hurdles, relaxed rhythm timing, a bath helper and tap-to-talk; and an Assistive Access scene with simple Feed, Water, Pet and Bedtime buttons.

## How it is built

- **Swift and SwiftUI**, with a **custom Metal renderer** and **soft-body physics** for the jelly bodies, a MetalFX upscaler, and frame rates up to 120 Hz on ProMotion displays.
- Apple frameworks: Speech and SoundAnalysis (on-device voice and listening), Foundation Models (on-device text), WidgetKit, ActivityKit, App Intents, Core Haptics, Core Motion and AVFoundation.
- No third-party dependencies.
- About 134,000 lines of Swift in the app, widget and shared code, with roughly 500 unit test files and 47 UI test files.

## Building and running

1. Open `BobV1.xcodeproj` in Xcode (an SDK that supports iOS 26.2 or later).
2. Select the `BobV1` scheme and an iPhone or iPad simulator or device, then run.
3. Tests live in the `BobV1Tests` and `BobV1UITests` targets. The physics suites run far faster in Release with testability enabled.

`scripts/phone-install.sh` is the checked route for a device build: it builds Release, verifies the bundle, and installs with `devicectl`.
